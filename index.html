How I can help.

Three areas of expertise, built around one conversation about what matters to you.

Private Wealth Management

I offer a complete range of wealth-management services, tailored to your unique financial planning needs. I provide the expertise, personalized care and resources to help you meet your financial planning, investment and estate planning needs. This includes will drafting, deceased estate administration, estate planning, life cover, critical illness, disability, income protection, bond protection, state cover, impairment cover and funeral cover, as well as local and offshore investments. Our goal is to learn what matters most to you and to understand the life you want to live, so that we can help you live your best life today and preserve and transfer your wealth to the ones you love.

Corporate & Employee Benefits

I offer comprehensive pension fund, provident fund and group retirement annuity solutions to our client base. Each employee benefit scheme, whether existing or new, receives a full analysis in terms of appropriate structure and investment advice.

Asset & Wealth Management

Considered investment and asset management to grow, diversify and preserve your wealth, locally and offshore, in line with your goals.
